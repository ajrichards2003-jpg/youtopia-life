## Conversion release steps
1. Apply under Youtopia Life with the live site, business/payment details and audience information. Record each programme's terms and approval. Do not auto-accept terms without owner review.
2. Select one specific product per partner, inspect quality evidence, warranty, AU shipping/returns and current price, and use a text-only product card pending creative permission.
3. Add clearly labelled commission disclosure close to the link, plus a site-wide statement; use tracking URL supplied to the approved account only. Check destinations and mobile checkout.
4. Track clicks and conversions through the partner dashboard; do not promise income from ordinary links.
5. Keep supplement pages conservative: no treatment/prevention promise, safety context, and no children's supplement merchandising.

## Printify first owned product
Account registration and Stripe verification remain with owner. Choose exact tee variant and provider; get production, shipping, taxes and fees for Australian checkout. If the owner wants a 30% gross margin on the full customer amount, target customer price = all-in variable cost / 0.70. If shipping is charged separately, calculate the margin on the combined checkout amount and disclose shipping clearly. Compare the final AUD-equivalent customer total to similar Printify storefront T-shirts, proof artwork, order a sample, and only then add an actual buy link.
