# Apparel IP and supplier release playbook

Version 1 · 29 September 2026 · Youtopia Life / Limitless Youth

This is an operating checklist, not a legal clearance opinion. Keep one rights record for every mark, design, image, slogan, font, product photograph, film and music recording used in a product or ad. “Attribution” is not permission, and a printer's upload acceptance is not rights clearance.

## 1. Decide exactly what is being sold

Record the supplier, blank garment model, colours and sizes, print method, placement and dimensions, retail price in AUD, shipping to an Australian test postcode, returns contact and physical proof. Label concept imagery as concepts until a real garment sample is photographed. For the first drop, the candidate Tee Junction blanks are AS Colour Staple Tee, Women's Maple Tee, Supply Hood and Women's Supply Hood. Adam accepts the platform's preset tee margin for this initial release; record the actual checkout and seller payout rather than claiming 30%.

## 2. Build a source file for every creative asset

| Field | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|
| Asset ID and use | Exact file/hash; tee print, website, ad, packaging or social post |
| Creator and human contribution | Names, dated sketches, vector layers, edits and approval history |
| Inputs | All photos, prompts, references, fonts, stock and model/voice sources |
| Tool and plan | Account, plan tier and terms effective when the output was generated |
| Licence | Commercial use, merchandise, modification, sublicensing to printer, territory and term |
| Third-party consent | Written permission for likeness, logo, portrait, song, footage or supplied creative |
| Final master | SVG/PDF/PNG dimensions, colour profile, transparent background and print proof |
| Search and sign-off | Trademark search date/results, legal review where needed, owner final approval |

Keep source files, exports, receipts and permission emails in a dedicated brand-rights folder with read access for the business team. Never assume a later paid upgrade cures restrictions attached to a free-plan output.

## 3. Separate use rights, copyright and trade marks

A platform contract may permit commercial use of an AI output without proving that the output has exclusive copyright under Australian law. Australian copyright generally arises automatically for an original work; there is no government copyright registration or “approval” certificate. Commission a human designer or make substantial human-authored vector design decisions, retain drafts, and get a written assignment/licence from any contractor covering apparel, advertising and future territories. A simple slogan or brand name is chiefly a trade mark question.

Search IP Australia's Australian Trade Mark Search and TM Checker for **YOUTOPIA LIFE**, **YOUTOPIA**, **LIMITLESS YOUTH**, close spellings and visually similar wing/Y devices. Review live and pending marks in clothing/headwear (class 25), retail (class 35) and relevant editorial/media services, plus unregistered market use, company/business names and domains. A search is a screen, not a guarantee. A trade mark professional should assess confusing similarity, correct owner/entity and classes before an application or scaling apparel. Do not put ® next to Youtopia or Limitless Youth unless registered for that mark; use another brand's ® only when naming its product accurately.

## 4. Clear the first-drop art

The existing wing PNG is a useful concept and print reference, but its AI-assisted origin does not establish exclusive copyright or similarity clearance. Do not claim it is a vector. Prepare a new editable vector master with deliberate human-authored geometry, black and white wordmark variants, colour specifications and a monochrome print version. Record who made each change and obtain written assignment if anyone outside the business contributes. Check the final art at actual left-chest size; get Tee Junction's print proof and one sample.

For the site, keep official supplier videos in their own players, link to publisher pages and use original summaries. Do not rehost or extract commercial films or images without express rights. The Free HeyGen narration and unverified welcome MP4 were removed from the preview branch; the replacement launch scenes use CSS and text. Check every new generation service's current plan-specific commercial terms **before** production.

## 5. Release record

One row per SKU: product page screenshot, final art hash, source/permission register, trademark review, supplier proof, sample photo, AUD cart including freight, payout calculation, care/size/returns copy, and owner sign-off. Archive the version of each item that actually went on sale. Reopen the checklist whenever the artwork, supplier, territory or ad use changes.
