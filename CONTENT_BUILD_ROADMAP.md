# Youtopia depth and product programme

Started 17 October 2026 after the journal design launch.

## Working rule

Build and test each next edition in a fresh preview. The design launch is approved; further content additions remain reviewable before publication. A source-backed feature distinguishes reported findings, editorial interpretation, product claims and practical guidance. Do not invent interviews, expert review, testimonials or study details.

- Expanded adult sleep guide: original reporting and practical context, three checked official source pages, a printable 14-day diary, symptoms that warrant assessment and links to related reporting.
- Field Guides landing page: three clear starting paths and a direct link to the expanded sleep resource.
- Product-panel contrast correction after launch, keeping the paper palette readable.
