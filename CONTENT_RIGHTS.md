# Youtopia Life content rights register

Reviewed 28 September 2026. This is an operational record, not a legal opinion. The site should not claim that attribution alone grants a right to use a photo.

## Current controls

- Articles and guides use original Youtopia summaries and link to research or supplier pages. Do not copy their full text, diagrams or product photography into Youtopia pages.
- Unsplash images used in the editorial pages are permitted for most commercial website use under https://unsplash.com/license . Retain photo URL or ID in source for provenance; do not resell the image on its own.
- Original third-party YouTube videos are linked or opened in YouTube's embedded player. Do not download, re-edit, rehost, strip player controls or use clips in advertising without permission. See https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780 .
- Commercial supplier, media and social-profile photos that had been hotlinked on the homepage were replaced by Youtopia text graphics in this branch. Product names link out to their official pages.
- Publisher book-cover images from Open Library's covers endpoint were replaced with Youtopia typographic graphics in this branch. Publisher book pages remain linked.
- The Commons file named `Naveen-Jain.jpg` depicts a different person (identified as a Rajasthan public-health official by its uploader); its use for the Viome founder was removed from homepage and profile.

## Provenance still to document

- Confirm creation or licence records for all locally hosted image assets, especially generated portrait/merch scenes and Youtopia-branded graphics. User supplied some Grok concept boards; Grok's consumer FAQ permits commercial use of outputs and requests attribution: https://x.ai/legal/faq . Preserve the generating account, prompt and original export where possible. A generated output can still resemble third-party protected material.
- Confirm generation or music licence for `assets/youtopia-ambient-original.mp3` and `assets/youtopia-ambient-still.mp3`, and source footage and soundtrack of the two locally hosted MP4 films. No licence or source record appears in this repository, so their rights are unverified in this audit. Keep source/project files and licence receipts.
- Before Printify publication, confirm the Youtopia wing mark and T-shirt print artwork are owner-created or licensed for merchandise, and check that the final print files do not incorporate another brand's logo, photo or garment mockup.
- For any new Commons file, verify its exact author and individual licence. A Commons URL alone is not a blanket site licence.
- For supplier partnerships, obtain written permissions for product media and usage scope, separately from affiliate tracking terms.

## Release gate

Do not merge this branch or the content/store preview solely on this checklist. Inspect the visible previews, confirm provenance for locally hosted media, and obtain qualified Australian IP advice for brand/trade-mark clearance and any doubtful third-party content before a commercial merch launch.
