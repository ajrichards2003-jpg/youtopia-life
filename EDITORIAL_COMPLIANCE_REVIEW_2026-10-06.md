# Youtopia editorial and commercial review
6 October 2026 (Melbourne) · Proposed corrections, preview first

## Outcome and scope
The evidence-first tone, sources and clear limitations are a strong foundation. They do not constitute legal clearance. Under TGA guidance, the overall presentation and direct or indirect promotion of a therapeutic good matter; an educational disclaimer, lack of commission, or source link is not an exemption. Prescription medicine promotion to the Australian public is restricted. A scientific study and a permitted product advertising claim are different questions.

Reviewed local text for all 14 Research/Frontier articles, the homepage, Edge, peptide map, supplement timing guide, Kids, iHerb collection, Shop, privacy and Store. This is an editorial risk screen with targeted source checks, not a clinical review of every statement, an ARTG clearance of every SKU, a legal opinion, or a rights audit of every media asset. Research dates already on articles remain unchanged unless an individual research update is made; new bylines do not claim professional clinical review.

## Higher-risk content assessment
- **Nicotine and cognition:** already says MCI is not Alzheimer’s dementia, no self-treatment, dependence harms and trial limitations. No nicotine purchasing path or regimen was found in the reviewed article/Edge text. Keep discovery headlines factual and avoid promotional ads.
- **Sildenafil:** circulation article separates mouse NMN work from a human sildenafil trial, states the primary trial result was not significant, and gives no regimen or purchasing path. US labels establish their own jurisdictional facts, not Australian registration. A TGA-experienced reviewer should assess the whole public context, including homepage teasers.
- **IV vitamin C/cancer:** NCI summary supports a small adjunct pancreatic study and differing results, with oncology safety context. This does not establish a general cancer treatment. Do not add infusion-clinic referrals, cancer-treatment sales copy or consumer dosing.
- **Peptides:** existing map distinguishes specific approved indications, investigational compounds and jurisdictions, and avoids self-injection instructions. Exact product registration and current warnings still need ongoing checks; no commercial peptide supplier link should be introduced.
- **Nattokinase/spike protein:** existing copy correctly separates a laboratory exposure experiment from swallowing a capsule and states that human clearance/long-COVID claims are unproven. Bleeding/anticoagulant cautions remain. Do not use those claims in product advertising.
- **Red light/HBOT/sauna:** evidence is outcome- and protocol-dependent. Keep targeted clinical work separate from consumer devices and systemic longevity claims. A consensus is not device approval; observational mortality associations are not causal proof.
- **Children:** reviewed Kids page is food-first, age-aware and routes supplement decisions to the child’s clinician. Do not add adult product links or extrapolate adult trials into child dosing.
- **Ultrahuman:** reviewed campaign copy frames estimated sleep and heart-rate trends as wellness insights, not diagnosis. Confirm offer validity, reference AUD price and actual checkout before each promotion; commission disclosure must remain beside offer links. This review does not independently validate the device or confirm tonight’s promotional eligibility.

## Launch decisions still requiring evidence
1. A TGA-experienced legal/clinical reviewer should assess public prescription, cancer and device/product-adjacent pages and the full advertising context. This is a specific unresolved review, not a claim that independent education is prohibited.
2. Every sale or affiliate product needs exact regulatory/product/claim evidence; retailer availability and ingredient literature are insufficient.
3. Brand/apparel rights remain open. The 29 September internal search recorded close marks YOOTOPIA 2581079, YOUTOPIA 2190260 and LIMITLESS 1947946/2502960. Current registry detail could not be retrieved through search on this review; no infringement conclusion or current legal status is asserted here. Give the dated records and final logo to an Australian trade mark attorney before public apparel listings or stock commitments.
4. Check exact image, film and music licences against the asset register. Official embeds and linked original sources reduce rehosting risk, but attribution alone is not permission.
5. Verify the deployed first-collection form and collection/retention process before promoting it. It must not automatically subscribe people to the newsletter.
