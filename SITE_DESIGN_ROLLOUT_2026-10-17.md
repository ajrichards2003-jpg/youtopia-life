## Checks and limits

Checked local image, stylesheet, script and link targets across all 66 HTML files. Homepage integrity passes. Existing scientific claims, product availability and commercial arrangements are not newly researched or re-certified by this design pass.

Browser layout and interactive end-to-end testing are not available in this environment. The static checks establish markup preservation and link integrity, not that every viewport or interaction has been visually verified. Review the preview before production.

Existing portrait credits and licence links are preserved. No new portraits, endorsements, supplier relationships or product claims have been introduced.
