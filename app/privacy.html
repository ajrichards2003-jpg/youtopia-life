Youtopia Life does not require an account, measure health data, or include analytics or advertising trackers in this app edition.

What stays here

Your saved article IDs, Ascent personal best and unfinished game are stored on this device. They are not sent to Youtopia. Memory-match and breathing activity state lasts only for the current session.

Offline reading

Native editions bundle the articles, games and artwork. In the browser edition, Enable offline reading downloads these files to browser storage. You can remove browser downloads in settings. External sources and website features require internet.

Clear your data

Use Clear reading list and game progress in settings to remove the app’s saved article IDs, Ascent best and unfinished run. Uninstalling the native app normally removes its device-local data; platform backup and browser storage settings can also affect retention.

External websites

Sources, product pages and other Youtopia website features open outside this app experience. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Website analytics consent is separate from app storage.
