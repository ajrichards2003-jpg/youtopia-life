Can we make our blood vessels younger?

A new human trial found improved blood vessel function within an hour of a longevity supplement. Six months later, three vascular health markers had improved. What does that really mean?

Study brief · Youtopia Life editorial

Every organ depends on the vascular system. As we age, the arteries often become stiffer and the delicate lining inside our blood vessels — the endothelium — may become less effective at regulating blood flow. These changes are relevant to cardiovascular health. But could a nutritional intervention make measurable differences to the way vessels function?

Researchers at the University of Surrey investigated that question in the STAMINA study, published on 4 November 2026 in European Heart Journal Open. The trial tested NOVOS Core, a commercially available multi-ingredient powder. It offers an encouraging signal, with important limits.

The one-hour signal

After the initial dose, blood vessel function was assessed using flow-mediated dilation (FMD), an ultrasound-based test of how much an artery widens when blood flow increases. At one hour, the adjusted difference between the supplement and placebo groups was 3.9 percentage points (95% confidence interval: 2.7–5.1).

That is a rapid change in a physiological measurement — not evidence that arteries literally became younger in an hour. Short-term changes in the ability of a vessel to expand are different from structural changes to ageing tissue.

Six months later

The trial enrolled 61 generally healthy, non-smoking adults aged over 40. Thirty-three were randomly assigned to the supplement and 28 to placebo. Forty-three completed both visits. Neither the investigators nor the participants knew their assigned group during the blinded study.

At six months, the supplement group had favourable changes in FMD, pulse wave velocity (a measure of arterial stiffness), and systolic blood pressure compared with placebo. Blood lipid levels and diastolic pressure did not change significantly. The estimated 10-year cardiovascular risk difference was modest, −0.45 percentage points, largely explained by the blood pressure finding.

The important distinction

These are intermediate measures of cardiovascular function. The trial did not demonstrate fewer heart attacks, strokes or deaths, or an increase in human lifespan.

Why a multi-ingredient formula?

NOVOS Core contains glycine, magnesium malate, calcium alpha-ketoglutarate, glucosamine sulfate, rhodiola, L-theanine, vitamin C, fisetin, hyaluronic acid, ginger, pterostilbene and a small amount of lithium. Researchers selected these compounds for potential relevance to ageing-related pathways.

There is a scientific rationale for investigating several processes together, but the study did not establish that the ingredients acted synergistically, that all ageing pathways were altered, or which compounds drove the observed effect. Magnesium, for example, has an existing human research base for blood pressure, particularly in people with hypertension or deficiency. Fisetin and calcium alpha-ketoglutarate are still emerging areas of human ageing research.

Where we should be cautious

  • 18 of 61 participants did not complete both visits (about 30% attrition).
  • One site, a small sample and generally healthy adults limit generalisability.
  • Adherence was self-reported; the trial could not establish sustained effects after stopping.
  • NOVOS supplied the products and contributed research funding; one author was an employee of NOVOS.
  • The study was not designed to establish long-term disease prevention or safety across different medical conditions.

What this means for you

These results are worth following, but are not a reason to substitute a supplement for proven approaches to cardiovascular health or prescribed treatment. Physical activity, a nutritious dietary pattern, managing blood pressure and not smoking remain important priorities. If you're already taking supplements or medicines, discuss potential overlaps and interactions with a pharmacist or clinician before adding a multi-ingredient product.

Youtopia evidence rating · Promising

A peer-reviewed human trial with a meaningful signal across several vascular markers. Replication, longer follow-up and clinical outcomes are still needed. The evidence rating applies to the vascular measurements in this trial.

Keep asking better questions.

Can a multi-target nutritional intervention improve the way our blood vessels function? This study suggests it might. Whether that means we can meaningfully alter the course of cardiovascular ageing is the next question.
