A new way to study ageing inside a clinical trial.

Could a disease-focused trial reveal something about ageing too? Protein-based clocks offer a new way to ask.

By Youtopia Life editorial · AI-assisted research and writing
Source checked 17 October 2026 · No independent clinical review · Editorial standards

Exploratory clinical-trial analysis

What the researchers found

Researchers applied six protein-based ageing clocks to samples from a 12-week phase 2a rentosertib trial in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. The clocks predicted lower biological age in treated groups, but could not separate ageing effects from disease-specific effects.

Where the evidence stops

This exploratory analysis supports further investigation. It does not demonstrate rejuvenation, longer life or suitability of the experimental medicine for healthy people.

The Youtopia perspective

The opportunity is in better trial design: collecting measurements that help researchers ask several useful questions at once. We will watch whether future work connects these biological signals with benefits people can feel and outcomes clinicians can measure.
