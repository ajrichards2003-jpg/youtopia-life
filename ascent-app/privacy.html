What stays here

Your Ascent personal best and unfinished game are stored on this device. They are not sent to Youtopia.

Offline reading

Native editions bundle all 30 questions and game assets. In the browser edition, Enable offline play downloads these files to browser storage. You can remove browser downloads in settings. External sources and website features require internet.
