How it works

Everything is connected.

Your body is made of systems that constantly work together. The brain sends signals, the lungs bring in oxygen, the heart moves blood, and the digestive organs make nutrients available.

Try this: choose the heart, then the lungs, to see how two different organs support the same big job.

Important: This is an early, G-rated educational preview, not a medically precise anatomy atlas. Illustrations show approximate organ locations, sizes and shapes and omit many structures.
