How we share comedy

These links open videos on the creators' or authorised channels. We credit the performer and source, write our own commentary, and do not download, re-edit or upload their routines. Where an official YouTube embed is enabled, we may show it through YouTube's player with its controls and attribution. For a Youtopia-branded compilation, short clip, soundtrack or advertisement, we will first get a licence from the rights holder.

“Best medicine” is our playful heading, not a health claim. We will keep expanding this collection with Australian voices and original interviews.
