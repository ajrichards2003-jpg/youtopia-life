15-minute daily workout

A simple, scalable daily session for body and mind.

← All field guides

Minutes 0–2: warm up

March in place, roll the shoulders and do gentle hip hinges. Start easy enough to talk comfortably.

Minutes 2–12: two rounds

Work 40 seconds and transition 20 seconds for each move: sit-to-stand or squat; wall or counter push-up; brisk march or step-up; hip hinge or glute bridge; plank at a wall or bird dog. Repeat the five moves once. Choose a pace and range that feel controlled.

Minutes 12–15: finish

Walk slowly for two minutes, then breathe and stretch gently for one. On low-energy days, use the full 15 minutes for a comfortable walk. Progress by adding a third round or separate walks when ready.

How it fits the week

Fifteen minutes every day is a useful start, but 105 minutes a week of moderate activity is below the general adult target of at least 150 minutes. Add walks or other aerobic movement, and include muscle-strengthening work on at least two days. Stop for chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness; get individualized advice if you have a relevant condition.
