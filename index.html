How we think

“Curious enough to investigate. Rigorous enough to question. Independent enough to change our minds.”

We don't assume something is true because it is popular, and we don't assume it is false because it isn't. We trace claims to their sources, look for independent evidence and conflicts, explain uncertainty, and keep digging when a question deserves it. We don't tell you what to think. We give you more to think with.

AI × human potential

New tools for harder questions.

AI can map proteins, predict parts of genome function and help researchers sift complex biology. A prediction is a starting point for experiments and clinical validation, not a diagnosis or a promise of better health.

Genomics · September 2026

AlphaGenome Atlas

DeepMind's map predicts molecular effects of DNA variants. The team explicitly cautions against using AlphaGenome for personal genome prediction.

Read the original research update →
Molecular biology

AlphaFold

Structure predictions can guide laboratory work on proteins and molecular interactions. They do not themselves establish a therapy's safety or benefit.

Explore the original project →
Clinical caution

Keep humans in the loop

Health AI can make confident errors. For diagnosis, medication decisions and urgent symptoms, use a qualified clinician and validated care pathways.
