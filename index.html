How we think

“Curious enough to investigate. Rigorous enough to question. Independent enough to change our minds.”

We don't assume something is true because it is popular, and we don't assume it is false because it isn't. We trace claims to their sources, look for independent evidence and conflicts, explain uncertainty, and keep digging when a question deserves it. We don't tell you what to think. We give you more to think with.
