## Production direction
- First colourway: black garment / light neutral artwork.
- Premium, minimal, lifestyle-brand positioning; avoid promotional-merch styling.
- Prefer embroidery only after a physical sample proves the mark remains legible at stitch size.
- Screen print / high-quality DTG are suitable initial decoration candidates; final method depends on supplier blank and sample.
- Keep print artwork as vector wherever possible.
- Preserve clear space around the Youtopia mark.
- No AI-generated version of the logo may be used as production art.
- Lifestyle/mockup imagery may be AI-assisted, but it must not be treated as the print master.
- Before sale: verify garment supplier commercial terms, print dimensions, colour profile, care-label requirements, Australian consumer/product requirements, and trademark clearance for wording/marks.

## Mockup shot list
For each style: clean front product shot, rear shot where relevant, close logo/detail shot, one natural lifestyle shot, one store-card crop. Maintain consistent lighting and art placement across men's/unisex and women's cuts.

## Store positioning
Collection name: LIMITLESS YOUTH — Collection 01
Tone: understated, optimistic, premium.
Core idea: Youtopia is more than merch; the garments carry the philosophy of living longer, brighter and more deliberately.
