## Artwork decision
The original full logo has 318.5 ppi at 100 mm and about 398 ppi at 80 mm, without upscaling. At 300 ppi its maximum native width is 106.17 mm. However, it includes very small lettering and visible edge remnants. A supplier must inspect it on black at actual size. Fine image detail matters more than a nominal dpi tag.

The preferred left-chest design is the wing only. The recovered original includes the wing plus lettering; the existing small web wing is NOT a production master. Ask a production artist/printer to isolate the exact wing cleanly from the original, without changing its shape, then supply a written proof and native pixel dimensions. Do not auto-trace or regenerate the brand and call it the original. Keep the original file unchanged. Embroidery should wait for an intentionally prepared stitch-safe version and physical sew-out.

## Economics
Adam’s acceptance of Tee Junction’s preset margin for the first tee supersedes the older 30% target for this initial item. Prior account settings recorded 25% wholesale markup, mathematically 20% of item retail before deductions. Confirm the current account and exact decorated quote before sale. Supplier public guidance describes monthly PayPal payout; payout is not configured in the last verified account record.

A public Tee Junction designer showed $19.95 for a default white Medium blank on 5 October. This is NOT a black decorated quote, a storefront wholesale cost, delivered sample total or approved retail price. Public shipping guidance does not settle the conflicting $6.95 designer/earlier storefront figures. Do not set a customer price from that blank figure.

Formula once costs are known: with supplier cost B and 25% markup, item retail P = 1.25 × B; apparent item commission = P − B = 0.20 × P. Net payout subtracts actual fees/deductions. Delivered customer total adds separately confirmed shipping and tax. Do not represent a 20% item commission as a 30% all-in margin.

## Shortest path to a real launch
1. Resolve the material similar-name findings with an Australian trade mark attorney using the dated records and final art. This is not a finding of infringement, but it is more than a routine empty checklist.
2. Obtain the exact clean wing source and supplier proof at 80 mm target width on black. Compare the full-logo 100 mm candidate only as an alternate proof, not the already-approved design.
3. Obtain a written one-unit decorated quote, AUD delivered sample total, current commission/payout terms, care/size data and confirmed unpublished/private product workflow.
4. Get owner approval of the concrete proof, sample size and delivered cost before payment. No sample has been ordered in this pass.
5. Review real sample fit, print hand feel, edges/colour and wash durability. Photograph the real garment for product images.
6. Configure payout and verify a test cart, delivery/returns responsibilities and complete product specifications. Then request final listing approval and link the real store.
