## Store copy

Name: Youtopia Ascent

Apple subtitle: Climb with curiosity

Google short description: Choose your climb: discovery, science and evidence. Play, practise and learn.

Description:

How far will your curiosity take you? Youtopia Ascent is a discovery, science and evidence question game with 60 questions across three modes. Kids (suggested 7–12) offers 15 friendly discovery questions and lets players continue after mistakes. Teen (13–17) offers 15 science, reasoning and online-safety questions. Adult (18+) selects a fresh 15-question climb from 30 health and evidence questions. No date of birth or identity is collected.

Use three lifelines: narrow to two, reveal a clue or take a second chance. Protect a rank every three correct answers, bank your progress or reach Summit Mind. Practice mode lets you learn every question without ending the run after a mistake.

Every answer has an explanation and a source link. Resume an unfinished run and keep your personal best on your device. The native app includes all questions for offline play; external source links require internet.

No entry fee, cash prizes, account or app analytics. Ranks represent game progress, not IQ, brain age or clinical ability. Youtopia Ascent is educational entertainment, not a health assessment or personal medical advice.

## Review notes

No login required. Start Ascent, choose an answer, lock it in and continue. The three lifelines can each be used once per run. Teen and Adult challenge mistakes end at the protected checkpoint. Kids mistakes continue; all practice modes continue and list missed questions. Closing/reopening the app provides Continue saved run. Settings can clear the best and saved run. Native content is bundled. Outbound source and website links first show a grown-up multiplication check, then open via the native browser bridge. Cancel or an incorrect answer keeps the player in the game.
