## Store copy

Name: Youtopia Ascent

Apple subtitle: Climb with curiosity

Google short description: Climb 15 health and evidence questions, practise and learn from every answer.

Description:

How far will your curiosity take you? Youtopia Ascent is a health, wellness and evidence question game. Begin a fresh 15-question climb from a 30-question bank. Questions move from foundations to interpreting study designs and fictional risk examples.

Use three lifelines: narrow to two, reveal a clue or take a second chance. Protect a rank every three correct answers, bank your progress or reach Summit Mind. Practice mode lets you learn every question without ending the run after a mistake.

Every answer has an explanation and a source link. Resume an unfinished run and keep your personal best on your device. The native app includes all questions for offline play; external source links require internet.

No entry fee, cash prizes, account or app analytics. Ranks represent game progress, not IQ, brain age or clinical ability. Youtopia Ascent is educational entertainment, not a health assessment or personal medical advice.

## Review notes

No login required. Start Ascent, choose an answer, lock it in and continue. The three lifelines can each be used once per run. A wrong challenge answer ends at the last protected checkpoint; practice continues and lists missed questions. Closing/reopening the app provides Continue saved run. Settings can clear the best and saved run. Native content is bundled; source links open via the native browser bridge.
