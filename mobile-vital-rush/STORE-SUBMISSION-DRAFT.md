Description:

A little fuel. A big adventure. Explore an original three-lane arcade world with quick controls and a colourful food theme.

Move between lanes, jump low hurdles and dodge tall walls. Collect avocado, apples, carrots, eggs and beans. Ten food pickups trigger Glow Rush: your explorer grows, glows and earns double pickup points for eight seconds. Shields protect against one collision, while blue research tokens add bonus points.

Learn with a 35-second practice run that never takes a life. Choose gentle pace, optional sound and less motion. Chase your own best, try a repeatable daily route and earn new suits by collecting food in regular runs. No penalties for taking a break: pause whenever you need, and the game pauses when you switch away.

Your scores, food total, suit and pace/motion preferences stay on your device. Native gameplay works offline. No account, ads, purchases, cash prizes or online leaderboard.

Food, glowing growth and research tokens are fictional game mechanics, not nutrition advice, substances to consume or health treatments.

## Review notes
No login or server needed. Tap LET’S RUSH; use the visible lane and jump buttons or swipe sideways/up. Desktop keyboard controls are also supported. Low hurdles can be jumped; tall walls require changing lanes. Regular runs have three lives. Glow Rush protects against collisions. Ten food pickups fill the energy meter. Research tokens are not consumed. Practice lasts 35 seconds, preserves lives and does not change records/unlocks. Daily route uses the device’s local date; regular/gentle bests are separate. Gold unlocks at 30 food; Violet at 80. Pause/resume and finish-run are available. App background events pause the native game. Portrait phone layout, all iPad orientations, and pause on resizing; verify on physical phone/tablet before submission.

Privacy & support is linked in the footer and notes. Reset scores requires an explicit in-game confirmation. Mail support links are behind a grown-up arithmetic check. This gate is not age verification or legal parental consent.
