Peptides, without the fog.

Peptides include established medicines and experimental compounds. The molecule, route, indication and jurisdiction determine what can responsibly be said. This is a living map of widely discussed names, not a claim that every peptide in research has been catalogued.

Read the status first. An Australian prescription medicine has an assessed indication and product information. A registered trial tests a question. A bottle sold online with “research use” on it is not proof of human safety, quality or benefit. We describe trial doses only when they help interpret evidence, never as self-injection instructions.
Metabolic medicines

Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy), liraglutide (Victoza/Saxenda), dulaglutide (Trulicity), tirzepatide (Mounjaro)

How it works / what is being studied
Approved Australian prescription medicines for specific diabetes and/or weight-management indications. GLP-1 signalling affects insulin, glucagon, appetite and gastric emptying; tirzepatide also acts at GIP receptors.
Timing, dose, combinations and higher-dose claim
Dose, schedule, titration and combinations are product- and indication-specific. Higher dose is not automatically better. Gastrointestinal effects, gallbladder/pancreatitis concerns and current TGA warnings need a prescriber.

Metabolic trial

Retatrutide

How it works / what is being studied
Investigational triple GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist studied for weight and metabolic outcomes. Trial data are promising, but it is not an approved Australian product.
Timing, dose, combinations and higher-dose claim
There is no Youtopia self-use dose or timing. The TGA names unapproved retatrutide among products raising safety and supply concerns; track named trials and peer-reviewed outcomes.

Recovery claims

BPC-157, TB-500

How it works / what is being studied
Promoted for tissue repair and injury recovery; mechanistic and animal work should not be mistaken for demonstrated human clinical benefit.
Timing, dose, combinations and higher-dose claim
The TGA identifies these as examples of unapproved products. No validated human dose, timing, combination or higher-dose benefit can be recommended.

Growth-hormone axis

CJC-1295, ipamorelin, sermorelin, tesamorelin

How it works / what is being studied
These are not interchangeable. They act at growth-hormone release pathways; tesamorelin has a specific U.S. prescription indication for excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV-associated lipodystrophy, not general anti-ageing.
Timing, dose, combinations and higher-dose claim
Australian registration must be checked by exact product. CJC-1295 appears on TGA unapproved-product warnings. Dose and monitoring belong to a clinician and the relevant approved label; watch glucose and IGF-1 context.

Skin and pigmentation

GHK-Cu, melanotan II

How it works / what is being studied
Copper peptides are studied in skin and wound contexts; melanotan products aim to alter pigmentation. A cosmetic ingredient and an injectable therapeutic claim are different questions.
Timing, dose, combinations and higher-dose claim
TGA flags unapproved GHK-Cu products. Do not infer safe injection or a reliable higher-dose benefit from topical research; verify route, formulation and trial quality.

How it works / what is being studied

Each needs an individual indication, jurisdiction and trial-status review before an evidence claim. This list is for research triage, not an endorsement or dosing menu.
