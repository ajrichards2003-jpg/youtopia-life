Privacy at Youtopia Life.

Updated 18 October 2026. This page explains the information we collect when you use youtopialife.com and how to contact us about it.

What you give us

When you submit our newsletter, contact or first-collection interest forms, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, chosen interests, size preference or message. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive information through these forms. We use submissions to respond to you or provide the update you requested. Newsletter consent and first-drop interest are separate choices.

Service providers

Our site is hosted by Netlify, which processes form submissions and technical requests for us. If you follow a link to a supplier, video platform, social network or merchandise store, that service handles its own site, checkout and privacy practices. We do not receive card details from those external checkouts. YouTube preview images and externally hosted editorial images can send a technical request to their providers when a page loads, before you play a video. Playing an embedded video loads the provider’s player and its privacy practices apply.

Analytics and device storage

We use Netlify Web Analytics to understand traffic through hosting-server logs, without placing analytics cookies or loading a browser analytics tag. This update removes Google Analytics and its optional consent banner; navigation, supplier-click and video events are no longer sent to Google Analytics. Public campaign labels in links may be carried between our pages and included with a form you choose to submit, so we can identify the source of that enquiry or subscription. Our existing Awin affiliate links may include a public placement label for aggregate click and sales reporting. These labels contain no visitor identifier, and this feature uses no cookies, browser storage or additional tracking requests. Netlify processes technical requests to operate and measure the site. The optional soundtrack stores your track choice in your browser. Brain games may store a best score on your device. You can clear local storage and any cookies from earlier visits using your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep form information only while needed to answer your request, manage a requested subscription or understand first-collection interest, then delete it under our operating process. Ask us to remove your submitted details at any time. We will also honour unsubscribe requests for marketing email. Hosting providers may retain technical records under their own policies.

Your choices and questions

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of information you supplied, or withdraw newsletter consent, by emailing info@youtopialife.com. Please include enough context for us to identify the submission without sending sensitive details. We may need to verify a request before acting. We will update this notice when our tools or practices change.
