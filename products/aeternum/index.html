Before checkout

Four details worth checking.

01

The exact product

Read the current label, ingredients, serving size and warnings. Match any research you read to the actual formulation rather than the category name.

02

The documentation

If testing matters to your choice, ask for a certificate of analysis for the batch you would receive and the identity of the testing laboratory.

03

The final cost

Check the checkout currency, Australian delivery eligibility, shipping charges and any recurring order settings. We haven’t confirmed a Youtopia customer discount.

04

The return terms

Read the current supplier policy for your destination before ordering, including conditions for opened products and international return postage.
