Evidence before expectations

Muscle stimulation needs context.

The FDA’s consumer guidance says electrical muscle stimulators may temporarily strengthen, tone or firm muscles, but do not establish weight-loss or dramatic appearance promises. General evidence about a technology does not verify an individual branded device.

We have not independently verified APE BORN’s model-specific clearance, clinical results or Australian regulatory status. Calorie-burn figures, rapid transformation claims and comparisons with exercise are not established by this introduction.

Before you choose

Check the details that matter.

Exact model and evidence

Ask for the model’s instructions, intended use, regulatory identifier and supporting research. Match any claimed clearance to the actual device and permitted use.

Suitability and instructions

Read contraindications, placement guidance and warnings. If you have an implanted electronic device or a medical condition, ask your treating clinician before considering use.

Total cost

Check what the kit includes, accessory costs, warranty and return conditions. Review any membership trial, recurring charge and cancellation terms before checkout.

Australian purchase

Confirm checkout currency, shipping, duties where applicable, local support and the exact model’s Australian regulatory status. These details have not been independently confirmed here.
