Before you choose

Six questions worth asking.

1. What are you scanning?

Small, textured objects, large parts and human subjects can need different tools and conditions.

2. What accuracy is required?

Read model-specific manufacturer specifications and test results. No scanner is equally suited to every material or distance.

3. Does your computer qualify?

Confirm processing, graphics, operating-system and storage requirements for the specific scanner and software version.

4. Which formats can you export?

Check mesh formats, workflow integrations, post-processing requirements and any software licensing or subscriptions.

5. What about privacy?

Get informed permission before scanning people, and decide where models and personal data are stored or shared.

6. Who supports the purchase?

Confirm Australian shipping, taxes, warranty, returns, local support and any import costs directly with the supplier.
