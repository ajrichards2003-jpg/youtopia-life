01 / The perspective

Style begins
with you.

One small detail can change the whole composition.

Sunglasses sit at the meeting point of personal style and everyday practicality. A sharp edge, a softer curve, a little more scale: each creates a different impression, without asking you to change who you are.

OTRA describes its approach as Australian design with sculptural shapes and considered releases. Our edit starts there, then asks the useful questions: how does a frame fit, where will you wear it, and what does the individual product actually specify?

Less scrolling.

The walk before work. A weekend away. An afternoon with no particular plan. Our interest in eyewear belongs to a wider idea: the things we choose should have a place in the life we actually live.

That means thinking beyond a campaign photograph. Choose for comfort and your own routine, then check the frame’s specifications and the retailer’s current terms.

04 / Before you choose

The details make
the difference.

Fit first.

Compare lens width, bridge and temple measurements with a pair you already find comfortable. Check the return conditions before ordering.

Read the lens.

Check the specific pair’s lens category, stated UV protection and any use restrictions. Do not infer these details from colour, darkness or styling.

Check the region.

Confirm your delivery country, currency, shipping cost and returns route on the retailer’s site. Current availability can vary by market.

Keep it current.

We have not fixed a price or discount into this edit. Use OTRA’s live listing for current prices, promotions and stock.
