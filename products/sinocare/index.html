Before checkout

Look beyond the device price.

The right model

Confirm the intended use, display units, instructions, phone compatibility where relevant, and which accessories are included.

The ongoing cost

Check replacement strip and lancet compatibility, pack quantities, expiry dates and availability. For blood pressure devices, check cuff sizing.

Buying from Australia

Confirm shipping costs, delivery estimates, returns, warranty support and the exact model’s Australian regulatory status. We have not yet independently verified TGA status for individual models.

A useful conversation

If you are buying for a medical condition, ask your treating clinician which device and monitoring approach fits your care plan. Follow the product instructions and your clinician’s advice.
