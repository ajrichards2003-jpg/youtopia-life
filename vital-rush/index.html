FUEL YOUR NEXT LEVEL.Original game · Playable prototype

Find your flow.

Collect colourful foods, including eggs and beans for the protein theme. Every ten food pickups fill your fictional energy meter and trigger an eight-second Glow Rush with double pickup points. Shields absorb one collision. Jump low hurdles; change lanes around tall walls.

Blue research tokens earn game points. Their peptide-inspired symbol is a fictional research collectible, not a substance to consume or a treatment. Growth, energy and scores are arcade effects, not claims about health or body size.

Three lives, no time pressure to restart, no ads, purchases, accounts or online leaderboard. Your best scores, food total and suit choice stay in this browser. Daily routes use your device’s local date. Gentle and regular runs have separate best scores.

Desktop: arrows/A/D move, Space/↑ jumps, Escape pauses. Touch: swipe sideways/up or use the buttons. Switching away pauses your run.

This prototype is for testing the fun, controls and replayability. Top-10 downloads are a goal, not a forecast.
