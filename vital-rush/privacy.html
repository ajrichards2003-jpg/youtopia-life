Privacy & support

Youtopia Vital Rush · version 1.0 · 7 October 2026

Your game stays on your device

The game stores personal-best scores, food totals, your selected suit and pace/motion preferences locally. No account, date of birth, health measurements, contacts, location, camera or microphone are requested. There are no ads, purchases, analytics SDKs or online leaderboards in the game.

Use “Reset scores and unlocks” in the game notes to clear your records. Clearing app/browser storage or uninstalling may remove them. Device backup and browser settings can also affect retention. Records do not sync between devices.

Downloads and connections

The native iOS and Android editions bundle the game and play without an internet connection. In a browser, opening or optionally downloading the game contacts its hosting provider, Netlify, which processes ordinary web requests such as IP address and browser information. Browser offline play downloads game files, not user records. Optional offline downloads can be removed using your browser’s site-data settings.

Family play

Food, shields, glowing growth and research tokens are fictional arcade mechanics, not nutrition advice, substances to consume or health treatments. Practice does not penalise collisions. No chat or user-generated content is available. Links leaving the game show a grown-up arithmetic check; it is a navigation barrier, not age verification or verified parental consent.

Support

For a bug, accessibility issue or privacy question, email info@youtopialife.com. Please include the game version, device/OS and what happened. Do not send passwords or sensitive health information.

Accessibility

Keyboard controls, visible touch buttons, pause, gentle pace and less-motion options are available. The moving canvas game currently requires sight; it is not fully playable with a screen reader alone.
