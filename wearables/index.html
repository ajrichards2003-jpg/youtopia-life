03 · Clinical context

Continuous glucose monitors

A CGM uses a small sensor to estimate glucose in interstitial fluid over time. These are medical devices for diabetes management; they are not a general score for how “healthy” a meal is. Eligibility and subsidies in Australia vary.

CGM · Australia

Dexcom

Explore current Dexcom systems, indications, alerts and Australian access. Discuss a sensor choice and readings with your diabetes care team.

Explore Dexcom Australia →

If you have diabetes or use medicine that can lower glucose, do not change treatment from a sensor trend alone. Follow your care plan and use a blood glucose meter when readings do not match symptoms or expectations.

A better purchase question

What will you do with the data?

Choose one question first: “Am I sleeping more consistently?”, “How is training affecting recovery?”, or “How is my glucose changing under my care plan?” Check fit, subscription cost, privacy, phone compatibility and whether a feature is available in Australia. The most useful device is one whose signals you can interpret and act on without chasing every daily score.
